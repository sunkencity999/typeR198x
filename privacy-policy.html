Type-R 198X

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 16 October 2026 · Applies to the web build at typer198x.com and the iOS/iPadOS and macOS builds.

Type-R 198X is a single-player typing shooter published by Christopher Bradford. The short version: the game keeps your progress on your own device, never sends it anywhere, and does not know who you are.

What is stored, and where

  • Save data stays on your device. Pilot name, ship selection, unlocked sectors, sector bests, high scores, settings (sound, CRT, motion) and any saved run are written to your browser's local storage, or to the embedded web view's local storage in the native apps, under the single key typesmup198x.save.v1.
  • One session flag. On phones, dismissing the "rotate to landscape" hint sets typer.rotateHintDismissed in session storage for that browser session only.
  • Nothing leaves the device. There is no server account, no cloud save, no leaderboard upload. The pilot name is only used to label your own records and HUD.

What is not collected

  • No personal identifiers: no email address, no account, no password, no advertising or analytics identifiers.
  • No telemetry, crash reporting, fingerprinting or IP-based tracking.
  • No advertising SDKs, payment processors or third-party scripts. The web build makes no network calls while you play beyond fetching its own art, music and code from typer198x.com.

Offline-first by design

The native apps bundle the whole game and run it offline inside a WKWebView. A connection is only needed to download or update the app from the store, or when you explicitly visit typer198x.com. Store purchases and distribution are handled by the platform storefront under its own privacy terms.

Your controls

  • Reset save on the title screen (below the stats panel) erases everything the game stored, after a confirmation.
  • Clearing your browser's site data for typer198x.com removes the same data; deleting the app removes it on iOS, iPadOS and macOS.

Children

The game is rated for ages 9+ (mild fantasy violence). Because nothing is collected from anyone, no data is collected from children under 13.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the effective date above is updated and the revised text is published on this page. Questions go to support@typer198x.com.
