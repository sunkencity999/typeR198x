Arcade-grade typing thrills

Type-R 198X turns deliberate practice into a neon space opera. Type the first letter of a target to lock on, finish the word to fire, and build accuracy and speed without staring at drills. Every keystroke is a shot fired.

Skill Builder

Graded letter sets and word pools, three announced waves per sector, live WPM and accuracy on the HUD, and a rank (S to D) on every results card that weighs accuracy, speed and hull.

Ten Sectors

Ten sectors over seven hand-painted skylines, each ending in a three-phase word-boss with typable minions and a telegraphed Signal Surge you can counter. Storm passes, a finale vista and a typewriter ending.

Seamless Progress

  • Saves stay on your device: ships, runs, sector bests, records.
  • Checkpoints every sector; retry a boss from the phase you reached.
  • Sector select replays any sector you have unlocked.

Powerups & Combos

SHIELD, MULTI, SPREAD, PIERCE, RAPID and HEAL capsules drift in; type their label to grab them. Combo tiers lift the multiplier up to ×5 (×7 with MULTI); a hard miss or hull damage resets it.

Perfect For

  • Writers and coders who want faster, cleaner typing.
  • Students needing a break from repetitive drills.
  • Arcade fans chasing the next legendary high score.

Plays Everywhere

Free in the browser (Safari 14.1+, Chrome 87+, Firefox 103+), with a fullscreen mode, a CRT filter, music and sound sliders, a reduced-motion option and screen-reader announcements. Touch devices type through the on-screen keyboard; a hardware keyboard gives the best experience.
