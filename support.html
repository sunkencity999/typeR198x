Type-R 198X Support

For support or questions email the creator at

support@typer198x.com
  • Lost progress? The game saves on your own device only (browser local storage, key typesmup198x.save.v1). Clearing site data or switching browsers starts a fresh save; nothing is kept on a server.
  • Starting over. Reset save sits in the link row under the pilot stats on the title screen and asks for confirmation first.
  • Stuck on the loading screen? The game needs Safari 14.1+, Chrome 87+ or Firefox 103+. If art fails to arrive, the loading screen offers Retry and Continue (procedural fallbacks).
  • Privacy. No accounts, no analytics, no tracking: see the privacy policy.
