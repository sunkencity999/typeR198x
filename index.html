        Mission briefing

        WordStorm Protocol

        In 198X, the world’s neon skyways are protected by the TypeGrid—an arcade-era defense network that keeps the city’s air-lanes stable by continuously “spelling” the right commands into reality.

        Then the WordStorm hits. Letters break loose. Words mutate into swarms. Boss-ships the size of buildings roll in like floating billboards, monorails, and glitching arcade cabinets—each broadcasting corrupted phrases that rewrite the sky into chaos.

        You are a rookie pilot plugged into the last working TypeGrid interceptor: the Keystream. Your weapon isn’t ammo—it’s accuracy. Every correct keystroke fires a laser. Every completed word shatters enemy code. Every boss is a living headline you have to type back into submission.

        Clear 10 sectors, reclaim the High Score, and punch through the final signal: the Electromancer Empress, source of the WordStorm, who’s trying to overwrite the world with a single unstoppable message.

        Type fast. Type clean. Save the city—one letter at a time.

        Pilot manual

        How to play

        Controls
        • Type the first letter of a target to lock on, then keep typing to finish it. Enemies win over powerups when they share a first letter.
        • Space or Backspace releases your lock. Three misses on a locked target also drop it. Wrong keys never cost points, but a hard miss breaks your combo.
        • Enter advances screens. Esc pauses or backs out. Alt+Enter (Cmd+Enter on a Mac) or F11 toggles fullscreen. M mutes on the title and pause screens. ? or F1 opens this manual.
        • Keys are ignored (never counted) during the sector card and a boss intro. Once you have beaten a boss before, any key skips its intro after one second; after a boss falls, any key, Space, Enter or a tap skips the finale after two seconds.
        • Keep typing through hit-stop and slow motion; input is never dropped while the playfield is live.
        Scoring
        • Letters pay 50 pts, words 60 + 12 per letter, and a word typed without a miss pays ×1.2. Minions pay 40, pickups 100, boss letters 10, a boss segment 30 per letter and a boss phase 300 × sector. Kills made by SPREAD, PIERCE or RAPID pay half.
        • Combo tiers: ×2 at 10, ×3 at 25, ×4 at 50, ×5 at 100. MULTI adds two tiers (cap ×7). A hard miss or real hull damage resets the combo.
        • Sector clear pays 1,500 + 350 × sector, plus bonuses for time under par, accuracy (90 / 95 / 98%), perfect waves, a flawless boss and countered surges. Retrying a sector scales those bonuses down by 25% per retry.
        • Your rank (S, A, B, C, D) weighs accuracy 50%, speed 30% and hull 20%.
        Powerups
        • Six capsules drift in, and kills drop them too (those home toward you). Type a capsule’s label to grab it; enemies win ties on the first letter. Shield and heal capsules turn up more often while your hull is low.
        • SHIELD (9 s) absorbs hits fully; each hit costs 3 s of shield, and shield time stacks up to 15 s.
        • MULTI (12 s) adds two combo tiers.
        • SPREAD (9 s): finishing a word also destroys enemies within 160 px.
        • PIERCE (9 s): each correct key also damages the enemy behind your target in the same lane.
        • RAPID (9 s): each correct key also hits every visible enemy whose next letter matches.
        • HEAL: instantly repairs 15% of your hull.
        Bosses
        • Each sector ends with a boss made of word segments in three phases (four for the finale). Type the highlighted segment; the lock is automatic.
        • Minions are typable. Their letters count for combo and score, not for wave quotas. A minion that reaches you costs 10 hull.
        • Signal Surge (from Sector 3): the boss charges a visible attack. Finish the current segment before the charge completes to counter it for a bonus; otherwise it hits for 8 + 2 × sector.
        • Clearing a phase grants 3 s of shield and a boss checkpoint. Once the finale starts, any key or a tap skips the rest after two seconds.
        Saving & sectors
        • Progress is saved on this device only. Every sector clear writes a checkpoint; dying returns your score to that checkpoint.
        • Resume continues a saved run. Replay restarts the sector you died in from its checkpoint. Retry boss restarts the boss from the phase you reached.
        • Sector select replays any unlocked sector. Only runs started from Sector 1 can set the high score; replays record a sector best instead.
        • Reset save on the title screen erases everything after a confirmation.
        Playing on touch
        • Tap the playfield to open the on-screen keyboard; letters fire as you type. A Bluetooth or USB keyboard gives the best experience.
        • Rotate to landscape for the full playfield. The Fullscreen button hides browser chrome where the browser allows it.
        • Non-Latin keyboard layouts and IME composition are not recognised. Switch to a Latin (QWERTY-style) layout or direct input.
        Accessibility
        • Settings (title screen or pause) hold music and sound sliders, mute, the CRT filter and its intensity, a motion option (follow system, reduced, full) and fullscreen.
        • Reduced motion removes shake, flashes, scanline roll and noise; screen changes become fades and count-ups land instantly.
        • Pauses, sector clears, deaths and boss arrivals are announced to screen readers. Every control shows a cyan focus ring.
